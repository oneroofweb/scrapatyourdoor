What changes the rate

The same item can get a slightly different price. Here's why.

Quality
Clean, dry raddi and pure copper get the top rate. Wet paper or mixed metal gets less.
Quantity
Bigger loads get a better rate per kg, because one trip covers more scrap.
Sorting
Paper, metal and plastic kept separately are priced higher than one mixed pile.
Market
Copper (tamba), brass (pital) and aluminium prices change almost every day with the metal market.
Condition
For ACs, fridges and washing machines, the size, brand, and copper or aluminium coil decide the price.

How we work out your payment

Every item is weighed on its own, on a digital scale, in front of you. We multiply the weight by the rate and write it on a slip. You get the total in cash or UPI before we leave.

No hidden cuts. No "we'll pay you later". What's on the slip is what you get.

Get a better price

Four small things that put more money in your hand.

Keep raddi dry

Wet paper weighs more but is worth less. Store newspapers in a dry corner and tie them in bundles.

Sort it first

Keep copper, brass, iron and aluminium apart. Mixed metal is always priced at the lowest rate.

Strip the wire

Copper wire without its plastic cover gets the full copper rate. With the cover on, it's priced lower.

Call before you sell

Metal rates move daily. A two-minute call tells you today's price, so there are no surprises.
