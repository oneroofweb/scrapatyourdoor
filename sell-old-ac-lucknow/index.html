What decides your AC's price

Two ACs of the same size can get different prices. These are the things we look at.

Tonnage
A bigger AC (1.5 or 2 ton) has more metal inside, so it gets more than a 1 ton AC.
Coil
Copper coils and copper pipes get a much better price than aluminium coils.
Compressor
An AC with its compressor still inside is worth more than one with the compressor removed.
Complete set
Both the indoor and outdoor units together get the best price.
Condition
A working AC can get more than a dead one. Broken or rusted parts bring the price down.
